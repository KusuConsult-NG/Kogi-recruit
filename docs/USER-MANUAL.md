# Applicant User Manual

Kogi State Civil Service Commission Portal

---

## Before you begin

You will need:

- a working **email address** you check regularly — every invitation and notice is sent there;
- a **Nigerian mobile telephone number**;
- **clear scans or photographs** of your documents, saved as PDF, JPEG or PNG.

**No payment of any kind is required** to register or to apply for any position advertised on this
portal. The Commission does not operate through agents or intermediaries. Report any demand for
payment to the Commission immediately.

---

## 1. Finding a vacancy

1. Open the portal home page.
2. Select **Recruitment** in the menu, or **View Current Vacancies** on the home page.
3. Open an exercise to read its description, eligibility criteria, application guidelines, the
   approved vacancies and the documents you will need.

Each vacancy states the job title, grade level, department or agency, the number of approved
posts, the qualification and experience requirements, and any age limits.

---

## 2. Creating your account

You need **one account only**, however many exercises you apply to.

1. Select **Create an Applicant Account**.
2. Enter your surname, first name, email address and telephone number.
3. Choose a password of at least eight characters including a letter and a number.
4. Read the privacy notice and tick the consent box.
5. Select **Create account**.

You are issued an **applicant reference number** in the form `KGCSC/APP/2026/000123`. Keep it.

### If you forget your password

Select **Forgotten your password?** on the sign-in page. A single-use reset link valid for sixty
minutes is sent to your registered email address.

### If your account is locked

After five failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for fifteen minutes. Wait, then try
again, or use the password reset link.

---

## 3. Completing your profile

Your profile is used on every application, so complete it once and keep it accurate.

Go to **My Profile** and supply:

- **Personal information** — surname, first name, middle name, gender, date of birth, marital
  status and your National Identification Number if you have one;
- **Contact details** — telephone number, an alternative number if you have one, your residential
  address, city and State of residence;
- **State and Local Government Area of origin** — choosing *Kogi* offers the 21 Kogi LGAs;
- **Disability or special requirements** — optional, declared only so the Commission can make
  reasonable arrangements.

Select **Save profile**. You cannot start an application until your profile is complete.

> Your email address cannot be changed yourself. Contact the Commission if it must change.

---

## 4. Starting an application

1. Open the exercise and select **Start my application**, or go to **My Applications → Apply for a
   vacancy**.
2. Choose **one** position from the list. Each position shows its qualification and experience
   requirements and any age limits — read them before choosing.
3. Select **Start application**.

You are issued an **application reference** in the form `KGCSC/2026/REC001/A1B2C3`. Your
application is saved as a **draft**: you can leave and return to it at any time before the closing
date.

Most exercises allow **one application per applicant**. The portal tells you if you have already
applied.

---

## 5. Filling in the application

The application page is divided into numbered sections, with a **completeness bar** showing how
much remains.

### 1. Personal and contact details
Taken from your profile. To change anything, edit your profile.

### 2. Educational and professional qualifications
**At least one is required.** For each, give the institution, the qualification obtained, the
course, your grade or class, the years attended and the certificate number. Add as many as apply.

### 3. Employment history and experience
Give each employer, the position held, the period and your key responsibilities. Tick *This is my
current job* where it applies. Optional, but it is how your experience is assessed.

### 4. Supporting documents
Each requirement states whether it is **required** or **optional**, the formats accepted and the
maximum size. Commonly required:

| Document | Format | Typical maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Passport photograph | JPEG or PNG | 2MB |
| Birth certificate or declaration of age | PDF, JPEG or PNG | 5MB |
| Evidence of National Identification Number | PDF, JPEG or PNG | 5MB |
| Educational certificates | PDF, JPEG or PNG | 8MB |
| Certificate of State or LGA of origin | PDF, JPEG or PNG | 5MB |
| NYSC discharge or exemption certificate | PDF, JPEG or PNG | 5MB |

Choose the file and select **Upload**. Each shows its review status. Uploading again replaces the
previous copy.

**If a document is rejected,** the officer's remarks say exactly what is wrong. Upload a corrected
copy and submit the application again.

> Scan at about 200 dpi, or save as PDF — this keeps files well within the size limits. Unreadable
> documents are returned for correction and delay your application.

### 5. Referees and next of kin
Up to three referees, each with a name, occupation, organisation and contact details. Next of kin
requires a name, relationship and telephone number.

### 6. Skills and additional information
List relevant skills, computer literacy and languages. Some exercises ask further questions here —
mandatory ones are marked.

### 7. Declaration and submission
Read both statements carefully, tick each box and select **Submit my application**.

The **declaration** confirms your information is true. A false statement or a withheld material
fact can disqualify your application or lead to the withdrawal of an appointment.

The **consent** allows the Commission to process your information for recruitment, under the
Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

If the button is unavailable, something mandatory is outstanding — the page lists what.

---

## 6. After you submit

You see an acknowledgement with your application reference, and the same confirmation arrives by
email and SMS. **Keep your reference number.**

Your application cannot be edited after submission, unless a screening officer requests a
correction — in which case it reopens and you are told what to correct.

### Withdrawing an application

While your application is a draft or newly submitted you may withdraw it from the sidebar.
Withdrawal cannot be undone.

---

## 7. Following your application

Your **Dashboard** shows every application and its current position. The **stage tracker** on the
application page shows where you are among the ten stages:

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 Recruitment planning | The Commission authorises the exercise |
| 2 Vacancy publication | Vacancies and guidelines are published |
| 3 Applicant registration | You create your account and profile |
| 4 Application submission | You complete and submit |
| 5 Preliminary screening | Officers check eligibility and documents |
| 6 Shortlisting | Qualified candidates are shortlisted and approved |
| 7 Examination | Where applicable, the written test is held |
| 8 Interview | Candidates are interviewed by a panel |
| 9 Final selection | Results are consolidated and authorised |
| 10 Closure | Applicants are notified and records archived |

What each status means:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Not yet submitted. Complete it before the closing date |
| Submitted | Received and awaiting screening |
| Under screening | An officer is reviewing it |
| Correction requested | Something must be corrected. Read the remarks and re-upload |
| Qualified | You met the criteria; shortlisting awaits approval |
| Disqualified | Not successful at screening. The reason is shown |
| Shortlisted | You are through to the next stage |
| Examination scheduled | Your examination number, date and centre are shown |
| Interview scheduled | Your interview date, time and venue are shown |
| Successful | You have been recommended for appointment |
| Unsuccessful | Not successful on this occasion |

### Tracking without signing in

**Track Application** on the public portal shows a summary from your application reference and
surname. Both must match.

---

## 8. Examination

Shortlisted candidates are notified by email and SMS, and the details appear on the dashboard:

- your **examination number**, which identifies you at the centre;
- the **date and reporting time**;
- your **centre and its address**, allocated in your own LGA where capacity allowed;
- your **seat number**, where one has been issued.

On the day:

1. Arrive **at least 45 minutes** before the reporting time.
2. Bring a **valid means of identification** and a **printed copy** of your invitation.
3. Mobile telephones and electronic devices are not permitted in the hall.

Your score appears on your dashboard once the Commission releases results.

---

## 9. Interview

Candidates invited to interview are notified with the date, reporting time and venue.

1. Bring the **originals** of every document you uploaded.
2. Bring a **valid means of identification**.
3. Arrive before your allocated reporting time.

A panel assesses you against the Commission's approved framework — job knowledge, communication,
analytical ability, conduct and overall suitability. Panel scores are averaged.

---

## 10. The outcome

Once the Commission approves the selection list, every candidate is notified.

**If you are successful,** you have been recommended for appointment. Formal documentation is
issued by the Commission. Final appointment remains subject to the approval of the legally
authorised government authorities and verification of your credentials.

**If you are not successful,** your dashboard says so, and you are encouraged to apply for future
opportunities.

---

## 11. Your notifications

**Notifications** keeps every message the Commission has sent you, by email, SMS or in the portal,
with the date it was sent. Nothing is lost if an email is mislaid.

---

## 12. Keeping your account secure

- Never share your password. The Commission will never ask for it.
- Change your password from **Change password** if you suspect it is known.
- Official messages come only from addresses ending **@kogicsc.kg.gov.ng**.
- The Commission charges **no fee** at any stage.

---

## 13. Help

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A file will not upload | Check the format and size stated for that document. Rescan at a lower resolution or save as PDF |
| Submit is unavailable | Something mandatory is outstanding. The page lists what remains |
| I chose the wrong position | Withdraw the draft and start a new application, before the closing date |
| I did not get the email | Check your spam folder. Every message is also in **Notifications** |
| My account is locked | Wait fifteen minutes or use the password reset link |
| Someone asked me for money | Report it to the Commission at once with any evidence |

For anything else use **Contact** on the public portal. Quote your application reference. Every
enquiry is logged with a reference number and routed to the responsible officer.

## Finding vacancies by ministry

Every advertised post belongs to a ministry, extra-ministerial department,
agency, parastatal, board or commission of the Kogi State Public Service. The
Commission conducts the recruitment; the MDA is your prospective employer.

Two routes are available:

- **Ministries & Agencies** in the main menu lists every MDA, with its mandate,
  its accounting officer, its departments and the number of posts it currently
  has advertised. Each entry links straight to its vacancies.
- On **Recruitment**, the filter at the top of the page narrows the exercises
  and the posts shown to a single MDA.

Each advertised position states the employing MDA and, where it has been
recorded, the department inside it where the post sits.

## Sitting an aptitude test or examination online

Where the Commission requires a test, you may be invited to sit it online in
this portal. **Tests and Examinations** in your portal menu lists everything you
have been invited to.

### Before you begin

The page states, for each test: your examination number, the time allowed, when
the test opens and when it closes. Read any instructions shown.

Make sure before you begin that you have:

- a steady internet connection;
- a quiet place where you will not be interrupted;
- enough battery or a power supply for the full duration.

**The clock does not stop once you begin.** It runs for the stated number of
minutes whether or not your browser is open, and the test is submitted
automatically when the time is up.

### While you are sitting the test

- One question is shown at a time. Select your answer and choose **Save and
  continue**. Where a question says *choose all that apply*, select every option
  you consider correct.
- Your answer is saved on the Commission's server each time you move to another
  question. You will not lose answered questions if your connection drops.
- The **question navigator** on the right shows every question: filled where you
  have answered, plain where you have not, with a gold dot where you have
  flagged it. Select any number to jump to that question.
- **Flag this question for review** marks a question you want to come back to.
  Flagging does not affect your answer or your score.
- **Save and go back** returns to the previous question. You may revisit and
  change any answer until you submit.
- The countdown is always on screen. It turns amber with five minutes left and
  red with one minute left.

### Submitting

Choose **Submit my test** when you are satisfied. You are warned if you have
left questions unanswered, and you cannot return to the paper once it is
submitted.

If the time runs out first, your test is submitted automatically and everything
you had answered is marked.

### After you submit

You are shown a record of your sitting: the number of questions on your paper,
how many you answered, how many you left blank, when you started and when you
submitted.

Your score is shown immediately only where the Commission has set the paper to
do so. Otherwise it is withheld, and you will be notified through the portal and
by email when the results of the examination are published. Officers validate
the scores for the whole examination before any result is treated as final.

Keep your examination number for any correspondence about the sitting.

### If something goes wrong

- **Your connection drops** — sign in again and open **Tests and Examinations**.
  If the clock has not run out, choose **Resume my test**; your saved answers
  are intact.
- **The clock ran out while you were disconnected** — the test is submitted and
  marked automatically from what you had answered.
- **The test will not open** — check the opening and closing times shown on the
  page. If the window is open and you still cannot begin, contact the Commission
  quoting your examination number.

No payment of any kind is ever required to sit a test. Report any demand for
payment to the Commission immediately.
